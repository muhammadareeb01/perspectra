Questions

Quick answers.

One at a time. Open what you need. Everything else stays out of the way.

Do you publish original news?
No. Perspectra is an aggregator. Snippets and comparisons link out to the publisher who did the reporting.
What does the bias bar measure?
For each story it shows the share of rated sources in the left, center, and right buckets. A bigger left slice means more left rated outlets picked it up. It does not mean the story itself is left wing.
How are outlets rated?
Lean and factuality are publication level labels from independent monitoring style research. They describe editorial tendency, not every journalist or headline.
What is a blindspot?
A story that skews hard to one lean, or where a whole side of the spectrum has few or no sources in the cluster yet.
Does the compass show story bias?
No. The compass mirrors the same source mix as the bias bar. It is a quick read of who showed up on this event.
How do I read the full article?
Tap Read on a channel card or use Read original on a story card. Links open the publisher site in a new tab.
